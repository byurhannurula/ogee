# OGee Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

OGee is a browser extension that inspects and displays the social sharing metadata (Open Graph, Twitter Cards, meta tags, links, and JSON-LD) of the page you are viewing.

## Data collection

OGee does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. Specifically:

- No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
- No network requests to any server. All metadata extraction happens locally in your browser.
- No account, sign-in, or identifier of any kind.

The optional export tools can open third-party sharing debuggers (Facebook, LinkedIn, X) in a new tab; those pages load only when you click them, and are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Settings

Your preferences (theme, panel position, per-site enablement, visible panel sections) are stored using the browser's extension storage on your device. Your browser may sync them between your own devices if you have browser sync enabled.

## Permissions

- `activeTab` and `scripting`: used only when you open the panel, to read the metadata of the current tab. OGee cannot read pages you do not inspect.
- `storage`: stores your settings locally.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published in this repository and reflected in the date above.
